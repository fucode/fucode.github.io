Bank of America Payment Types

A working reference for how money moves in and out of Bank of America accounts — the everyday tools for personal banking, and the treasury-grade rails used by businesses and commercial clients.

Most personal payments route through Online Banking or the mobile app, under Pay & Transfer. Zelle and Bill Pay cover almost everything day to day; ACH and wire exist for the cases where speed, amount, or destination require something sturdier.

Send & move money

Moving money to other people or your own accounts elsewhere.

Zelle®
SpeedMinutes
FeeFree
Send or request money using just a phone number or email — works with almost any U.S. bank, not only Bank of America. Best for splitting bills, paying a person back, or informal transfers between people who trust each other.

SpeedInstant

Move money between your own Bank of America checking, savings, and eligible brokerage accounts, or to another Bank of America customer's account.

Speed1–3 business days

Electronic transfer to or from an external bank account you've linked — the standard way to move money between Bank of America and an account you hold somewhere else.

SpeedSame business day

Deposit & receive

Getting money in — from a check, an employer, or another person.

Mobile check deposit
Speed1–2 business days
FeeFree
Photograph a check in the app to deposit it without a branch or ATM visit.
Direct deposit
SpeedPayday
FeeFree
Payroll, benefits, or government payments deposited straight into your account via ACH — often available up to a day or two early.
Zelle® (receiving)
SpeedMinutes
FeeFree
Receive money sent to your enrolled phone number or email; funds post directly to your account rather than sitting in a separate wallet.

Cards

Plastic (or digital) at the point of sale.

Debit card
SpeedInstant
FeeFree
Draws directly from checking; also usable for ATM withdrawals and linkable to Zelle, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Venmo.
Credit card
SpeedInstant
FeeInterest if carried
Bank of America-issued credit cards for purchases, with rewards programs and the option to pay in full or carry a balance.

International

Sending money outside the U.S.

International wire transfer
Speed1–5 business days
FeeFee + FX spread
Send funds abroad in U.S. dollars or foreign currency through Online Banking or a financial center; the main personal option for cross-border payments.

Business and commercial payments run through CashPro®, Bank of America's treasury platform, or Small Business Online Banking for smaller clients. The choice of rail is usually a trade-off between speed, cost, reversibility, and how much remittance detail travels with the payment.

Move funds

Core rails for sending money out of the business.

ACH (Automated Clearing House)
Speed1–2 business days
FeeLow, per-item
The workhorse for batched, predictable payments — payroll, recurring vendor payments, and collections. Cheap and reliable, but not instant and reversible within limits.
payrollvendor paymentsbatch
Wire transfer (domestic & international)
SpeedSame day
FeeHigher, flat
For high-value or time-critical B2B payments, including cross-border. Final and irrevocable once sent, which is exactly why it's used for large, one-off transactions.
high valueirrevocable
Real-Time Payments (RTP®)
SpeedSeconds, 24/7/365
FeePer-item
Instant, final, irrevocable payments on the RTP network, with limits up to $10 million. Used for vendor payments, emergency disbursements, payroll, and increasingly for deal closings — anywhere waiting for the next ACH batch or business day isn't good enough.
instantfinal on receiptnot reversible
Zelle for Business
SpeedMinutes
FeeFree / low
Small-business version of Zelle, with higher sending limits than personal accounts — used for routine payments to vendors, contractors, or customers who prefer a simple, fast transfer.
Recipient Select® / Digital Disbursements
SpeedMinutes–days
FeePer-item
Bank of America's own tools for paying individuals — rebates, insurance claims, gig payouts — by phone or email instead of collecting their bank account details.

Pay suppliers & payroll

Accounts-payable-specific tools that decide which rail a payment should ride on.

Complete AP
SpeedVaries by method
FeeProgram-based
End-to-end accounts payable automation: invoices are approved through the platform, then paid automatically by virtual card, ACH, or check — whichever the supplier prefers.
Virtual Payables Direct
SpeedFast, near-instant to supplier
FeeProgram-based
Lets a buyer make large, one-off, or last-minute B2B payments without the supplier needing any card-acceptance setup — the supplier gets paid quickly by bank transfer instead.
Check issuance & Positive Pay
SpeedDays
FeePer-item
Paper checks are still supported for suppliers who require them; Positive Pay matches every check presented for payment against what the business actually issued, to catch fraud before it clears.

Collect & deposit

Getting customer and payer money into the business.

Lockbox (wholesale & retail)
SpeedDaily processing
FeeProgram-based
Customers mail payments to a P.O. box Bank of America manages; the bank opens, processes, and deposits them, and converts paper payments to electronic where possible — used by businesses with high check volume.
Commercial & remote deposit
SpeedSame/next business day
FeeProgram-based
Quick Business Deposit for in-branch drop-off, or Remote Deposit to scan and deposit checks straight from the office.
Pay by Bank
SpeedInstant at checkout
FeeLower than card interchange
Lets a business's customers pay directly from their bank account at checkout instead of by card — simpler checkout, no card data stored, typically cheaper than card acceptance.
Intelligent Order to Cash / Receivables Matching
SpeedAutomated, ongoing
FeeProgram-based
Uses automation to match incoming payments to open invoices and manage deductions — cuts the manual reconciliation work that comes with high receivables volume.

Commercial cards

Card-based spend and payables.

Commercial / purchasing cards
SpeedInstant
FeeProgram-based, rebate-eligible
Corporate cards for travel and general spend, with the reporting controls a finance team needs to track who spent what, and often a rebate on volume.
Virtual cards (ePayables)
SpeedInstant to supplier
FeeRebate-eligible
Single-use, supplier-specific card numbers generated for individual payments — a way to pay vendors by card even when they don't take walk-up card payments.

Global & FX

Cross-border payments and currency risk.

International wires & global payments
Speed1–5 business days
FeeFee + FX spread
Cross-border B2B payments in local currency or U.S. dollars, with guidance for navigating destination-country requirements.
Transactional FX
SpeedTied to payment
FeeSpread-based
Currency conversion built into cross-border payment flows, for businesses that regularly pay or get paid in a foreign currency.

Platform & automation

The infrastructure that ties all of the above together.

CashPro®
Speed—
Fee—
Bank of America's global treasury platform — one place for payments, receivables, liquidity management, investments, FX, and trade, with real-time visibility across all of it. Most of the tools above are initiated or monitored through CashPro.
treasury hubused by 94% of the Fortune 500
Account Validation
SpeedPre-payment check
FeeProgram-based
Confirms a recipient's account status and ownership before a payment is sent — faster than older pre-notification or micro-deposit methods, and a straightforward way to cut fraud and misdirected payments.
