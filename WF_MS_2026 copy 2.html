01 • Business Overview

What Was RPA?

Risk Profile Assessment (RPA) supported Merchant Services Credit and Risk teams in evaluating merchants and determining whether their risk profile remained acceptable to the bank.

Merchant Assessment

Brought merchant information, processing activity, risk indicators and relationship information together for underwriting review.

Risk Evaluation

Credit Officers / Underwriters evaluated merchant exposure, risk rating, processing activity, collateral and other risk factors.

Credit Decision Support

The workflow helped collect and organize the information needed to support credit review, approval and case resolution.

02 • System Responsibilities

MSOW vs. MSCD

One of the most important architecture concepts was the separation between the workflow/UI application and the upstream merchant-data platform.

Upstream / Backend

MSCD

MSCD functioned as an upstream Merchant Services application and data store supporting merchant-data processing and risk analysis.

Key responsibilities:

  • Receive merchant-related data from upstream sources
  • Store merchant information
  • Support analysis used for periodic RPA reviews
  • Support alert-driven merchant reviews
  • Expose data and functionality through APIs
  • Serve MSOW as an API consumer
  • Support longer-term data-retention requirements
Data Store Upstream APIs Risk Data
UI / Workflow

MSOW

MSOW was the Pega-based application providing the user interface, case-management experience and underwriting workflow used to perform the Risk Profile Assessment.

Key responsibilities:

  • Consume MSCD APIs
  • Present merchant information to users
  • Create and manage RPA cases
  • Guide users through Pega stages and process steps
  • Support document-related workflows
  • Support approvals and case resolution
  • Maintain its own application/case data
Pega UI Layer Workflow API Consumer

04 • Business Workflow

Risk Profile Assessment Workflow

The RPA case guided the Credit Officer / Underwriter through the information required to evaluate the merchant and complete the assessment.

Pega concept

The overall case was organized into stages, with each stage containing process steps that moved the RPA toward completion.

1
RPA Case Creation

An RPA could originate from periodic merchant-review activity or other risk/alert-related activity requiring evaluation.

2
Merchant Review

The underwriter reviewed merchant profile information, processing activity, hierarchy and relevant risk information.

3
Risk & Exposure Analysis

The assessment considered risk exposure, processing activity, risk rating and other merchant-risk indicators.

4
Gather Additional Information

Users could request additional information from other groups, including the merchant's Relationship Manager, when more information was required.

5
Documents

Supporting documentation could be uploaded or referenced as part of the assessment and retained through the enterprise document-management process.

6
Bank Relationship Review

The underwriter could consider other Wells Fargo products or services associated with the merchant as additional relationship context during the assessment.

7
Collateral

Collateral information could be considered as part of the merchant's overall credit-risk assessment.

8
Credit Amount / Exposure

The assessment considered how much processing-related credit risk the bank was willing to assume for the merchant.

9
Approval

Where approval was required, the workflow supported identifying and routing to the appropriate approver.

10
Case Resolution

Once analysis, required documentation and approvals were complete, the RPA could move toward final resolution.

05 • Integrations

Supporting Systems

MSOW did not operate in isolation. The RPA workflow depended on integrations and enterprise services across Merchant Services and Wells Fargo.

MSCD

Backend merchant-data platform and API provider supporting MSOW.

ICMP

Enterprise document-storage capability used for application documents.

CAAMS

Database/system used to identify or look up appropriate approvers for approval-related workflow.

APIs

Integration layer allowing MSOW to consume data and functionality provided by MSCD and supporting services.

08 • Study Guide

Data & Architecture Terminology

These terms help explain the MSCD → MSOW architecture using common Product Owner, Business Analyst and data terminology.

Term How It Applied
Data Source Original system or provider from which merchant information originated.
Data Ingestion Process of bringing merchant information from source systems into MSCD.
Data Store MSCD stored merchant information required for analysis and downstream use.
Upstream System MSCD sat upstream of MSOW and supplied information required by the workflow.
API Provider MSCD exposed functionality/data through APIs.
API Consumer MSOW called and consumed MSCD APIs.
Transformation Changing the structure, format or representation of data so it can be processed or consumed appropriately.
Translation / Mapping Mapping values, fields or representations between systems so each system interprets the information correctly.
Data Lineage Understanding where merchant information originated, how it moved and which systems consumed it.
Data Governance Rules and controls governing data ownership, retention, quality and appropriate use.
