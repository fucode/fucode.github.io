Case Study 01 / Investment Technology

Modernizing Market on Demand (MOD)

React front-end modernization, investment research, reusable components, and investor experience improvements.

As part of a long-term, multi-Program Increment (PI) initiative, I supported modernization of the Market on Demand platform, including its Investment Screener and more than 9,000 investment fund profile pages.

01 — Situation

The legacy MOD platform was outdated, slow, and difficult to scale. TIAA needed a modern, responsive investment research experience supporting both retail and institutional investors.

02 — Task

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to modernize the platform, improve UI/UX, introduce new investor tools, and support migration to a React-based front end.

03 — Actions

  • Gathered business and technical requirements with Product, Design, Compliance, Accessibility, and Engineering.
  • Defined and refined epics, features, user stories, and acceptance criteria across multiple PIs.
  • Supported reusable React components and responsive front-end architecture.
  • Helped introduce interactive investor experiences, including a hypothetical $10,000 growth chart and dynamic fund comparison tools.
  • Facilitated demonstrations and usability reviews to incorporate stakeholder feedback.
  • Partnered with compliance and accessibility teams on Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) requirements.

04 — Results

The modernization delivered improved responsive design, reusable components, investor research tools, and a more maintainable platform. The original project account reported load-time improvements exceeding 60% and increased fund-profile engagement.

Case Study 02 / Institutional Retirement

PlanFocus 2.0: Institutional Platform Modernization

Redesigning retirement plan management experiences for sponsors and institutional partners.

I supported the multi-year PlanFocus 2.0 transformation, working with business, design, and engineering teams to modernize an institutional retirement management platform.

01 — Situation

PlanFocus served retirement plan sponsors but relied on aging workspaces, inconsistent navigation, and a fragmented user experience. Modernization was needed to improve usability, responsiveness, and operational efficiency.

02 — Task

Support redesign of legacy workspaces, define business requirements, clarify entitlement flows, and help introduce modern features for plan oversight, compliance, reporting, and education.

03 — Actions

  • Collaborated with Product, Engineering, UX, Legal, and Data teams on information architecture and reusable interface components.
  • Defined user flows and entitlement logic for role-based access across plans and institutions.
  • Supported redesign of plan dashboards, notifications, reporting, educational resources, and investment insights.
  • Participated in PI planning, backlog refinement, and roadmap alignment.
  • Coordinated UAT and acted as a liaison between business stakeholders and engineering teams.

04 — Results

The modernized platform improved access to plan information, administrative workflows, reporting, and retirement plan oversight. It supported institutional clients in making more informed decisions and managing participant-related responsibilities.

Platform Overview

Understanding PlanFocus

Plan sponsors manage complex retirement programs, regulatory responsibilities, participant information, reporting requirements, and employee engagement. PlanFocus brings these activities into a centralized institutional management experience.

Platform functionality described here reflects the experience documented in this portfolio and may differ from TIAA's current product.

My Plans

Plan-level dashboards, metrics, charts, and information supporting plan oversight.

For Your Attention

Time-sensitive tasks, compliance alerts, and reminders requiring sponsor action.

Plan Review

Participant trends, plan performance, peer benchmarks, and retirement readiness insights.

Report & Analyze

Reporting and aggregated information across multiple retirement plans.

Education & Resources

Educational materials, videos, webinars, and resources for employees and plan sponsors.

Investment Insights

Market commentary, investment perspectives, and information supporting plan monitoring.

Functional Reference

PlanFocus Detailed Platform Guide

Explore the primary functional areas of PlanFocus, including navigation, administration, user access, reporting, compliance, education, and document management.

Navigation Features
  • Tab Menu Bar: Access platform sections through navigation menus.
  • Search: Find authorized participants and relevant content, with predictive search results.
  • My Settings: Configure preferences such as alerts and prospectus delivery.
  • Contact Us: Access relationship management and service team information.
  • Notifications: Review time-sensitive tasks and action items.
Home Page Modules
  • My Plans — plan-level performance and metrics.
  • For Your Attention — important tasks and alerts.
  • Plan Review — readiness analysis and benchmarks.
  • Report & Analyze — aggregated plan information.
  • Resources for Employees — participant education.
  • Resources for Plan Sponsors — administration resources.
  • Investment Insights — market commentary and research.

Available features vary by user permissions and plan configuration.

The Administration tab supports essential retirement plan operations and administrative tasks.

  • Process contribution lists and remittances.
  • Review participant transactions electronically.
  • Add, remove, or update authorized site users.
  • Securely exchange plan and participant data through File Exchange.
  • Access forms for administrative and participant use.
  • Support employee enrollment and funding processes.
  • Monitor investment performance.
  • Manage Revenue Credit Account functionality where applicable.

PlanFocus provides user management and role-based permissions to protect sensitive retirement plan and participant information.

Core Capabilities
  • Add, edit, delete, deactivate, or reactivate users.
  • Copy permission settings from existing users.
  • Review a 12-month history of access changes.
  • Approve access for third-party firms.
  • Complete annual user access confirmations.
User Personas
  • Plan Sponsor / Location Sponsor: Broad plan administration and user-management access.
  • Responsibility Owner: Access and management within assigned responsibilities.
  • Access Rights Holder: Permissions limited to assigned actions.
  • External Users: Auditors, consultants, and other authorized professionals.
  • Legal Counsel: Users managed through the applicable administrator process.
Access Controls
  • Plan Scope: Assign access to specific or multiple plans.
  • Permissions: Edit & View or View Only.
  • Privacy: Restrict sensitive participant balances, compensation, and Social Security numbers.
  • Plan Review: Control access to salary-related reporting and benchmarking.
Functional Roles
  • Contributions and Enrollments.
  • Distributions and Loans.
  • Compliance and Fee Management.
  • Revenue Credit Account.
  • Plan and Investment Changes.
User Administration Workflow
  • Add / Copy: Enter user details and assign permissions, or copy an existing profile.
  • Edit: Update user profiles, plans, and assigned roles.
  • Manage Firms: Assign third-party access and expiration dates.
  • History: Review recorded access changes.
  • Annual Confirmation: Review and certify authorized access.

The documented registration process included a one-time personal identification number (PIN) provided outside the online registration flow.
