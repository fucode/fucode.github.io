TIAA
RetirePlus®

A model-based retirement investment solution designed to combine diversified asset allocation, automated portfolio management and the potential for lifetime income within an employer-sponsored retirement plan.

Product perspective • Business Analysis • Product Ownership

01 • Product Overview

What is RetirePlus?

RetirePlus is an asset-allocation offering that manages participant portfolios through investment models. Rather than functioning as a single investment, the product provides a framework for allocating retirement assets across multiple underlying investment options.

Model-Based

Participants are managed through asset-allocation models rather than selecting and manually managing every investment independently.

Diversified

Models can allocate across multiple asset classes and underlying investments, creating a portfolio designed around the plan's investment strategy.

Adaptive

Depending on the model, allocations can change over time as participants move closer to or through retirement.

02 • Product Architecture

How RetirePlus Works

From a product perspective, RetirePlus can be viewed as an orchestration layer between the retirement plan, investment models and underlying investment vehicles.

1
Plan Sponsor

Defines the retirement plan structure, available investment options and governance requirements.

2
RetirePlus Model

Applies the selected investment strategy, allocation rules and model structure.

3
Asset Allocation

Determines the portfolio's allocation across eligible asset classes and investment vehicles.

4
Participant

Receives a managed retirement investment experience aligned to the plan's selected model strategy.

03 • Model Strategies

Different Ways to Build the Model

RetirePlus supports different model structures depending on the plan's objectives and level of customization.

Strategy 01

Target-Date

Allocations gradually shift from more aggressive toward more conservative positioning based on an expected retirement year.

Retirement Year Glide Path
Strategy 02

Target-Risk

Maintains a defined risk profile, such as conservative, moderate or aggressive, rather than continuously changing toward a retirement date.

Risk Profile Fixed Strategy
Strategy 03

Years-to-Retirement

04 • Product Variants

RetirePlus Select vs. RetirePlus Pro

The major product distinction is the amount of customization available to the retirement plan sponsor.

Standardized

RetirePlus Select

A predefined model framework designed to provide a simpler implementation path for plan sponsors.

  • Predefined years-to-retirement models
  • Conservative, moderate and aggressive model sets
  • Plan investment options can be incorporated into the models
  • Designed for a simpler default-investment implementation
  • Model allocation framework developed using a third-party asset allocation methodology
Customizable

RetirePlus Pro

A more customizable platform that allows the plan sponsor and investment professionals to tailor the investment strategy.

  • Target-date model support
  • Target-risk model support
  • Years-to-retirement model support
  • Greater control over investment strategy
  • Customizable asset allocations and glide paths
05 • Investment Layer

The Model Is Not the Investment

One of the most important product concepts behind RetirePlus is the separation between the allocation model and the underlying investments.

The model determines how retirement assets should be allocated. The underlying investment options provide the actual vehicles used to implement that allocation.

Product architecture perspective

RetirePlus can therefore be understood as a portfolio-management and allocation layer sitting above the underlying investment layer.

Equities

Growth-oriented investment exposure.

Fixed Income

Income and portfolio diversification.

Real Estate

Real-asset exposure within the portfolio.

Guaranteed

Potential lifetime-income and guaranteed investment components.

06 • Participant Experience

From Investment Complexity to a Guided Experience

RetirePlus abstracts much of the investment allocation complexity from the participant. Instead of requiring every participant to continuously determine the ideal mix of investments, the model provides a structured portfolio strategy.

The participant experience is therefore less about manually selecting individual investments and more about choosing or being assigned an appropriate model.

1
Participant Enters the Retirement Plan

The participant becomes part of an employer-sponsored retirement plan with an available investment menu and model strategy.

2
Model Selection

The participant may be placed into or select an appropriate RetirePlus model depending on the structure of the plan.

3
Portfolio Allocation

The model determines how the participant's retirement assets are allocated across eligible underlying investment options.

4
Ongoing Portfolio Management

Depending on the model, allocations can be adjusted over time as retirement approaches.

5
Retirement & Income

RetirePlus solutions can incorporate lifetime-income investments, connecting the accumulation phase with the participant's eventual retirement-income needs.

07 • Retirement Strategy

Designed to Connect Saving With Retirement Income

A defining feature of the RetirePlus proposition is its ability to combine portfolio allocation with retirement-income considerations.

TIAA describes RetirePlus solutions as potentially QDIA-eligible, allowing employers to use the models within a qualified default investment framework.

The solutions can also incorporate lifetime-income investments, bringing TIAA's annuity capabilities into the broader retirement investment strategy.

QDIA + Lifetime Income

The product can help plan sponsors create a default investment strategy while incorporating investments designed to support retirement income.
