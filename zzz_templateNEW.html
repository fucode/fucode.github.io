Most personal payments route through Online Banking or the mobile app, under Pay & Transfer. Zelle and Bill Pay cover almost everything day to day — ACH and wire exist for when speed, amount, or destination need something sturdier.

Business and commercial payments run through CashPro®, Bank of America's treasury platform, or Small Business Online Banking for smaller clients. The choice of rail is usually a trade-off between speed, cost, reversibility, and how much remittance detail travels with the payment.
