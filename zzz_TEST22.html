Product Requirements

Information Architecture Matters

A fund profile has to present potentially complex financial information in a way that is organized, understandable, and useful to the intended audience.

  • Organize information into logical sections.
  • Clearly communicate important investment attributes.
  • Consider how users navigate from search results into detailed information.
  • Align content and functionality with business requirements.
  • Coordinate requirements across business, UX, content, and development teams.
  • Validate that the delivered experience reflects the intended product requirements.
Product Design Principle

The goal is not simply to display more information. The goal is to present the right information at the right point in the user's investment research journey.

Product / Business Analysis

Content Is Part of the Product

For financial services websites, content and functionality are closely connected. The Product Owner must understand how content contributes to the overall customer journey.

  • Define content and page requirements.
  • Consider reusable page structures and components.
  • Coordinate requirements between business, content, design, and development.
  • Support page-level acceptance criteria.
  • Validate content presentation and functionality.
  • Consider responsive digital experiences.

Why AEM Experience Matters for a Product Owner

Working with AEM provided exposure to the intersection of product requirements, content management, user experience, reusable components, and enterprise publishing workflows. It reinforced that a digital product is more than its underlying technology — the content and experience delivered to the user are part of the product itself.

11 • Experience

What This Experience Taught Me

Financial services products require a Product Owner to understand more than screens and requirements. The underlying business process, information, content, and user journey all matter.

  • Financial products require careful handling of complex information.
  • Search and filtering are product capabilities, not merely UI features.
  • Information architecture can determine how effectively users understand financial data.
  • Content management is an important part of enterprise digital product delivery.
  • Reusable digital components can improve consistency and delivery efficiency.
  • Strong Product Owners connect business goals, users, technology, and delivery.
