02 / Product Portfolio

Two products. Two operational problems. One delivery mindset.

Both initiatives used Pega to create more structured, traceable workflows, but each solved a different business problem.

Product 01

Risk Profile Assessment

Internal underwriting and risk-monitoring workflow for Merchant Services Risk and Credit teams.

Risk & Compliance
  • Supported periodic merchant reviews across yearly, quarterly, monthly and custom review cycles.
  • Helped support the transition from a Fiserv-supported process to an internal Wells Fargo platform.
  • Worked with merchant information, risk exposure, collateral, ratings and credit approval information.
  • Supported risk indicators such as BQR, CQR, AQR, NDX, chargebacks and refunds.
  • Partnered with Risk, Credit, Compliance, Change Managers and development teams.
Product 02

Shared Mailbox Modernization

Pega case-management workflow designed to replace fragmented Outlook-based operational handling.

Operations & Workflow
  • Helped convert shared mailbox activity into structured Pega cases.
  • Supported email listener behavior, worklists, unassigned queues and routing rules.
  • Helped define relationships between ET cases and linked SR cases.
  • Supported visibility into action-needed items, close times and operational follow-up.
  • Partnered with Operations, Change Managers and developers through build, UAT and launch preparation.

05 / Delivery Approach

From discovery to production with feedback built into the process.

The Shared Mailbox initiative followed an iterative Agile delivery approach. Each sprint created an opportunity to build, test, demonstrate and refine the product.

1

Discovery & Stakeholder Alignment

Interviewed business partners, clarified the operational problem and documented desired workflow behavior.

2

Backlog Refinement

Broke down requirements into INVEST-style stories, clarified dependencies and prepared acceptance criteria.

3

Sprint Build & UAT

Supported incremental development and validated completed functionality through user acceptance testing.

4

Sprint Demo & Iteration

Demonstrated functionality to stakeholders, gathered feedback and refined the next set of work.

5

Final Signoff & Launch

Coordinated final UAT completion, defect resolution, business approval and production-readiness activities.

6

Warranty & Reporting

Supported post-launch follow-up, operational visibility and reporting needs.

06 / User Acceptance Testing

Making sure the workflow worked for the people using it.

UAT was more than checking whether a screen worked. It was about confirming that the complete business process supported the way Risk, Credit and Operations teams actually worked.

UAT Coverage
  • Confirmed entry criteria after QA and system integration testing.
  • Prepared test scripts mapped to user stories and acceptance criteria.
  • Validated happy-path, edge-case and permission scenarios.
  • Tested workflow routing, queues, notifications and business rules.
  • Coordinated daily updates, defect triage and retesting.
  • Supported final business signoff before production launch.
