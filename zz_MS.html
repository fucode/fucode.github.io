04 • The Two Applications

Risk Profile Assessment vs. Shared Mailbox

Same Product Owner, two very different problems — one an underwriting and risk-monitoring tool, the other a case-management workflow replacing manual email triage.

Underwriting & Risk

Risk Profile Assessment (RPA)

Internal underwriting and risk-monitoring tool for Merchant Services Risk & Credit teams.

  • Migrated merchant risk analysis off an external vendor (Fiserv) onto an internal platform
  • Defined the core domain model: merchant info, risk exposure, risk rating, collateral, credit approval
  • Shaped the risk rating framework and key exposure indicators (chargebacks, refunds, non-delivery)
  • Owned the Pega front end, consuming APIs and Kafka events from backend credit-decisioning
  • Upgraded merchant hierarchy from API V1 to V2 and expanded the processing-volume review window
Case Management

Shared Mailbox (SMB)

Case-management application replacing manual, Outlook-based email handling with structured workflow and tracking.

  • Migrated the shared Outlook mailbox into a Pega-based case-management solution
  • Addressed SLA breaches caused by manual triage and lack of case tracking
  • Defined the case model: Email Triage (ET) cases linked to Service Request (SR) cases
  • Improved auditability with structured assignment, escalation and reporting
  • Used the SMB foundation to stand up follow-on mailboxes, including Illinois tax/tip/fee routing

06 • SMB Delivery in Detail

Agile Delivery Process — Shared Mailbox

This delivery lifecycle applied specifically to the Shared Mailbox modernization: UAT after every sprint while building the MVP, sprint demos for stakeholder feedback, continuous iteration, a final UAT sign-off ahead of launch, and a post-launch warranty period with Grafana reporting to measure success.

1
Discover

Engaged Operations stakeholders to understand mailbox pain points, workflow challenges, volume and SLA risk before defining requirements.

2
Refine the Backlog

Translated discovery findings into INVEST-aligned user stories with clear acceptance criteria and prioritized the backlog for delivery.

3
Build & Validate

Delivered the MVP incrementally across sprints, with UAT performed after each sprint to validate functionality and identify gaps early.

4
Demo & Iterate

Demonstrated sprint increments to stakeholders, captured feedback, and refined the product and backlog through continuous iteration.

5
Sign Off & Launch

Completed final UAT, secured business stakeholder sign-off, and coordinated the production launch.

6
Warranty & Measure

Supported the post-launch warranty period for defects and enhancements, while using Grafana reporting to monitor adoption and operational results.
